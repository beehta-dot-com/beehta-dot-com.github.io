Privacy

What we collect. When you create an account, auth.beehta.com stores your email address and what is needed to sign you in. When you use an app, it stores the information you choose to put into it.

How it is used. Your data is used only to run the apps for you. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers and never used to build a profile of you.

Contact form. Messages sent through the contact form include your name, email and message. We use them only to reply.

Where it lives. Account and app data is kept with our hosting and database providers, who handle it on our behalf and only as needed to run the service.

Your choices. You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time. Deleting your account removes your data from every app.

Cookies

Staying signed in. auth.beehta.com uses a cookie or similar browser storage to keep you signed in across the apps. Without it you would have to log in on every page.

The background. Your arrangement of the background tiles is kept in your own browser, not on our servers. You can reset it from the footer.

No tracking. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking on beehta.com.
