Contact

Say something.

A bug, an idea for the next page or app, or a question about any of this.

It comes straight to us, you do not need an account, and there is nobody here whose job is to deflect you.

We use what you write here to answer you, and for nothing else. See Privacy.

Suggesting something

Describe the job rather than the software. Quite often the answer turns out to be a single page and not an app at all, which means it can be built in a weekend.

Reporting a bug

Three things help most: which page or app, what you did, and what happened instead. A screenshot beats a description.

How long we take

Usually a day or two. If it is a bug that is stopping you working, say so in the first line and it goes to the top.

Why there is no address here

An address written on a public page is on a spam list within a week. The form reaches the same inbox.

Not sending yet. The form checks what you type but has nowhere to send it until ENDPOINT is set in assets/js/contact.js. Working hours, and anything else worth listing here, are still to be decided.

Small pages for a single answer. Small apps for the work that comes back.
