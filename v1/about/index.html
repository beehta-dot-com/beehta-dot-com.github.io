What it is

beehta.com is a web ecosystem: a growing set of apps that live under one roof and one account. Each app looks after one kind of information, like your money, your subscriptions or your logins, and does that one job well.

Think of it as a personal assistant for the admin side of life. The bills, renewals, passwords, plans and lists that quietly eat into your evenings get a proper home, and you get that time back.

What it helps with

Some apps are ready today and more are on the way. Here is the kind of thing they cover:

  • Money: budgets, spending, savings and what you own.
  • Subscriptions: every plan you pay for, with renewals flagged before they charge you.
  • Logins: all your accounts and what each one is connected to.
  • Trips: bookings, day plans and costs in one place.
  • Notes and lists: the small things you would otherwise forget.

Built to be simple

Every app starts with one question: what is this for? Anything that does not help answer it stays out. That keeps screens calm, choices clear and the learning curve short.

If you need a manual to use it, it is not finished yet.

  • Simple: only what the job needs.
  • Purposeful: one app, one clear job.
  • Intuitive: you can find your way around without a tour.

Your way, not a template

Most apps hand you a fixed set of boxes and expect your life to fit inside them. We think it should be the other way round.

Apps on beehta.com let you add your own fields, rename things, choose how information is grouped and pick the views that make sense to you. Your setup can change as your life does, without starting over.

One account for everything

You sign in once and every app knows who you are. No separate sign-up for each tool and no extra passwords to remember.

Your data belongs to you. It is never sold, never used for ads, and you can ask for it to be removed at any time.

Where it is going

The goal is one place to handle all of your personal data, with apps that work together so something you enter once can be used wherever it helps.

New apps arrive one at a time, each built properly before the next one starts. You can see what is being worked on in the On the way section of the home page.
