What gets easier

The small jobs that take up a week.

Keeping track of things is rarely hard. It is scattered: across notes, messages, files and memory. beehta.com gives every piece of data a place and lets the apps pass it on.

  • Stop searching

    Whatever you saved, and however long ago, it has one place. So there is one place to look, instead of a search through notes, messages and old files.

    In practice Every value has a copy button beside it.

  • Enter it once

    What you add in one app is already in every other app that needs it. Nothing is typed twice, and no two copies drift apart.

    In practice One entry updates three apps. Try it below.

  • Stop keeping it in your head

    Anything with a date is shown before the day arrives, with the time left on it. The apps do the remembering.

    In practice Anything due within a week rises to the top.

  • Keep it your way

    Everything is a card, and you decide what is on it: add a field, rename a type, group by whatever was entered. There is no fixed format to fit into, for what you keep or for what you post.

    In practice Any field in use can group or sort the list.

  • Remember one sign-in

    One account opens every app. Start with the one that solves today’s problem and add another when it is needed.

    In practice Signed in to one app is signed in to all.

  • Take it anywhere

    Each app installs on a phone or a computer straight from the browser, and the data exports to CSV, vCard or JSON whenever it is wanted elsewhere.

    In practice An export is always a valid import.

Sharing

Share it in a moment.

Everything on beehta.com is private until its owner shares it. Board sends a card to the people who need it, puts favourite places on a map and shows what connections have shared.

Share a card

A packing list for the people travelling, a recommendation for anyone who asks. Choose who sees it, and take it back at any time.

  • With other users: chosen people, to view or to edit.
  • By link: anyone with the link can open it.
  • With the public: it appears on the owner’s public page.

Places on a map

The café worth the detour and the town worth the trip, saved where they are and shared with connections.

  • Food spots: where to eat and what to order.
  • Travel destinations: places visited and places to visit.
  • One map of everything connections have shared.

A feed of shared cards Planned

What connections share arrives in one feed, as on a social network. A post is a card, so it takes whatever shape its author gave it.

  • Cards shared with you, newest first.
  • Public cards from the people you follow.
  • Any card can be a post: a list, a place, a recommendation.

Privacy

What is stored, and what is not.

Advertising
None. Data is never sold and never used for advertising.
Tracking
None. This site loads no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.
Cookies
One, set at sign-in, to keep the session across the apps. Nothing optional is stored, so no consent is requested.
Passwords
Accounts keeps password hints. It never asks for a password and never stores one.
Demos
Sample data only. It lives in the page and is gone when the page closes.
Ownership
Every record belongs to one account, and the database enforces it on every table.
