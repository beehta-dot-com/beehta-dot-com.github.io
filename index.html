About beehta.com

How it works

Three steps, and one of them is optional.

  1. 01

    Have a look

    Every app on this page has a stand-in you can use straight away, with no account and nothing to install. If it is not for you, you have lost a minute and given away nothing.

  2. 02

    Sign in once

    If one of them turns out to be useful, a single account covers it and everything else on the shelf. There is one sign-in page, and no app ever asks for a password of its own.

  3. 03

    Open the real thing

    You land back where you started, signed in. Closing your account later closes it everywhere at once, in one place, without an email exchange.

Where this stops

What you will not find here.

  • No adverts

    Not on this page, and not inside any app on the shelf.

  • No selling what you put in

    What goes into an app stays in that app. It is not a product and it is not for sale.

  • No account maze

    One sign-in covers everything. There is no upgrade prompt waiting behind a button you did not press.

  • No features for their own sake

    If a thing does not earn its place on the screen, it comes off the screen.

Check these before publishing. Those four are promises. Keep only the ones every app will honour.
