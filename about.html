What this is

Most software grows until it does everything badly. The idea here is the opposite: build one small thing that does its job properly, put it on its own address, and leave it alone. When a second thing is needed it becomes a second app, not a new tab inside the first.

beehta.com itself holds nothing. It is a catalogue. The apps live on their own addresses and this page points at them.

One account, all of them

You sign in once, in one place, and that covers every app on the shelf. No app asks for a password of its own and none of them keeps its own list of users. There is a single sign-in page and everything else borrows from it.

So adding a new app does not mean another account to remember, and closing your account closes it everywhere at once.

Try before you sign up

Every entry in the catalogue has a small working preview. It is a stand-in, not the real app: fewer features, no saving, nothing sent anywhere. It is there so you can tell in ten seconds whether the thing is any use to you, without handing over an email address first.

What it does not do

  • No adverts, anywhere.
  • No selling or sharing of what you put into an app.
  • No pushing you towards a paid tier you did not ask about.
  • No feature that exists only to make a chart go up.
Check these before publishing. Those four are promises. Keep only the ones you are certain every app will honour, and see Privacy for what actually gets collected.

The background

The coloured squares behind this page are made from the colours of the apps in the catalogue. They can be moved: drag one onto another, or tap one and then a second, and the two swap places. Your arrangement is kept in your own browser and nowhere else, so it is there next time and nobody else sees it. There is a link at the bottom of every page to put it back.
