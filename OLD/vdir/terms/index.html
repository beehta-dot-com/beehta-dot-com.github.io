Terms of use

What this page is

beehta.com is a catalogue. It lists web apps, each of which lives on its own address and has its own terms. Using this catalogue means agreeing to what is on this page. Using one of the apps means agreeing to that app's terms as well.

What you can expect from us

We try to keep the catalogue accurate and the previews working. We cannot promise the site is available at every moment, and we may change, rename or remove an entry at any time.

What we expect from you

  • Do not try to break the site or get at parts of it that are not public.
  • Do not use the contact form to send bulk or automated messages.
  • Do not copy the site's design, wording or logo and present it as your own.

The previews

Each preview on the home page is a small stand-in written to show what an app does. It is not the app. Nothing you type into a preview is saved or sent anywhere, and none of the information shown in one is real.

Privacy

Last updated: not yet published

The short version

This page does not ask who you are. There is no sign-in on beehta.com, no account, and no tracking of what you read here. The only time you hand over anything is when you choose to fill in the contact form.

The contact form

When you send a message we receive your name, your email address, the subject you picked and what you wrote. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass it to anyone who is not helping us answer you.

Fill in before publishing. Name the service that relays the form, say where it stores messages, and say how long you keep them. This section is not honest until those three are answered.

The apps

Each app on the shelf handles your information under its own privacy notice, on its own address. Nothing you put into an app is visible to this page.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Use the contact form and we will do it as quickly as we can.

Cookies and storage

Last updated: not yet published

No cookies

This page sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising script and no third-party tag on it.

One thing kept in your browser

The site stores a single value in your own browser, using local storage rather than a cookie. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else: if you move the coloured tiles behind the page, where you put them is remembered so the page looks the same next time.

Clearing your browser's site data for beehta.com removes it. The link at the bottom of every page resets the arrangement without touching anything else.

If that changes

If anything is ever added that does track you, this section will say so before it is switched on, and you will be asked first.
